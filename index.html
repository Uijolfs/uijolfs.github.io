Between the world
and its models.

I am interested in how physical reasoning and mathematical models help us understand complex systems—what they reveal, what they leave out, and where they stop being useful.

What draws me to a problem also lies beyond the model itself: why it matters, and who might benefit from solving it. Clinical questions bring these interests together, connecting the search for understanding with the possibility of improving people's lives.

I am an undergraduate double majoring in Physics and Electrical & Computer Engineering at Worcester Polytechnic Institute. Before transferring to WPI, I spent two years studying physics at Lanzhou University.
